Privacy

Last updated: 26 November 2026

What this site collects

This portfolio does not run advertising, analytics, contact forms, account registration or a visitor database. Its own code sets no cookies. Contact links open your email app or the chosen external website.

Fonts and icons

Fonts and tool icons are served from this site. Your browser makes no requests to Google Fonts or other icon services.

AI comparison

Comparisons run in your browser from a bundled data file. No prompts or selections are sent to AI providers. Sharing a comparison puts the selected model or plan identifiers in the link. The Python updater runs separately on the owner's computer.

Hosting

The intended host is GitHub Pages. GitHub logs visitors' IP addresses for security purposes, even when they are not signed in. See GitHub Pages data collection and GitHub's privacy statement.

Presentation video

The video is hosted on Google Drive. The Google player loads only when you choose to play it. At that point your browser connects to Google, which may receive technical information and use cookies according to your Google settings and Google's privacy policy. The case study and slides work without loading the video.

Downloads and external links

The spreadsheets, SQL file and presentations are public project materials provided by David Alter. LinkedIn, GitHub and other external services have their own privacy practices. An email you send is handled by your email provider and the recipient's.
