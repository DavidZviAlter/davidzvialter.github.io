Privacy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

What this site collects

This portfolio does not run advertising, analytics, contact forms, account registration, or a visitor database. Its own code does not set cookies or store information in your browser. Contact links open your email app or the selected external website.

AI comparison

Comparisons run in your browser using a bundled data file. No prompts or selections are sent to AI providers. Sharing a comparison puts the selected model or plan identifiers in the link. The Python updater runs separately on the owner's computer.

Hosting

The intended host is GitHub Pages. GitHub logs visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes, even if they are not signed in. See GitHub Pages data collection and GitHub’s privacy statement.

Presentation video

The video is hosted on Google Drive. The Google player is loaded only when you choose to play it. At that point, your browser connects to Google, which may receive technical information and use cookies according to your Google settings and Google’s privacy policy. You can read the case study and browse the slides without loading the video.

Downloads and external links

The CV, spreadsheet, and presentation are public project materials provided by David Alter. External services, including LinkedIn and GitHub, have their own privacy practices. An email you send is handled by your email provider and the recipient’s email service.
